From web to app: architecture & bottom bar

What this is. We took the paths people actually follow today on the Rasmus mobile site and reorganized them into 5 fixed destinations in a bottom bar, instead of copying the hamburger menu. The idea is simple: not every flow is a tab. Bidding, for example, is something that happens inside a tab, not a tab of its own. Live sits in the bar because the business asks for it, and its main challenge, what to show when there is no stream, is flagged on the map.
